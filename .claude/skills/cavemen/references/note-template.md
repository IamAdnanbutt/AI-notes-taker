# Note Block Template

Under each annotated screenshot, write a note block using this EXACT
structure. Omit the TRADE SETUP block only when no trade is shown.

```
TIMESTAMP: [HH:MM:SS] | LINK: [YouTube URL?t=seconds]
ICT MODEL: [Which of the 7 models this belongs to]
CONCEPT: [Primary concept being shown e.g. FVG Retracement Entry]
TIMEFRAME: [e.g. M1 / M5 / M15 / H1 / H4 / D1]
INSTRUMENT: [e.g. XAUUSD / EURUSD / NQ / etc.]
SESSION: [London / NY AM / NY PM / Asian]

WHAT IS HAPPENING:
[2-4 sentences describing exactly what the chart is showing. Be specific:
mention candle direction, where the sweep happened, where MSS confirmed,
where FVG formed, what timeframe, and what the expected direction is.]

ICT PLAYBOOK RULE APPLIED:
[Quote the specific rule from the ICT Gold Notes Master Playbook that this
example demonstrates]

TRADE SETUP (if applicable):
- Bias: [Bullish / Bearish / Neutral]
- Entry trigger: [What confirmed the entry]
- Entry price: [exact or approximate price level]
- Stop Loss: [where and why]
- Target: [first target / final target / DOL]
- RR Ratio: [e.g. 1:3]

KEY LESSON:
[One sentence. The single most important thing to remember from this
screenshot.]
```

## Worked example

```
SCREENSHOT 1 — [00:04:32] → XAUUSD M5 FVG Formation During NY AM Kill Zone
[Annotated screenshot embedded here]

TIMESTAMP: 00:04:32 | LINK: youtube.com/watch?v=xxx&t=272
ICT MODEL: Model 2 — MSS + FVG Reversal
CONCEPT: Bullish FVG forming after SSL sweep and MSS confirmation
TIMEFRAME: M5 | INSTRUMENT: XAUUSD | SESSION: NY AM

WHAT IS HAPPENING: Price swept the Asia session lows (SSL) at the open of the
NY AM Kill Zone. After the sweep, a 3-candle bullish FVG formed on M5 followed
by a Market Structure Shift to the upside. Price retraced into the FVG at the
50% equilibrium level (OTE zone) offering the long entry.

ICT PLAYBOOK RULE APPLIED: After a liquidity sweep and MSS, enter on
retracement into the nearest FVG within the Kill Zone window.

TRADE SETUP:
- Bias: Bullish (Daily Discount, targeting BSL above)
- Entry: 2318.50 (50% of FVG)
- Stop Loss: 2314.20 (below the sweep candle low)
- Target: 2331.00 (Previous Day High / BSL)
- RR Ratio: 1:3

KEY LESSON: Never enter before the MSS. The FVG only becomes valid AFTER the
liquidity sweep and Market Structure Shift confirm the direction.
```
